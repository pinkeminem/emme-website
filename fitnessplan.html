Phase 1 Notes

Weighted hip thrusts from day one — not bodyweight. Start with a weight you can do 10 clean reps with and add load every week. Slow the tempo: 3 sec down, 1 sec squeeze at the top. You should feel the glute, not the quad or lower back.

Morning after a Tresor shift: yoga and walk only. Sleep and recovery beat one workout.

How the Diet System Works
01
Pick, Don't Plan
Hungry? Pick one from each list. Any combination works. No planning, no math, no tracking required.
02
Protein Anchor
Every meal has a protein source. If you improvise something, it must also have protein. This is the only rule.
03
Pre-Portion the Night Snack
Before you smoke: portion snack into a bowl. Non-negotiable. This single habit prevents 300–500 extra cal/night.
04
Garlic Oil Not Garlic
Garlic-infused oil is FODMAP-safe, raw garlic is not. Buy a bottle. Use it everywhere garlic is called for.
05
Eating Out in Berlin
Sushi (salmon/tuna, no onion sauce) · Döner bowl (extra meat, hold sauce) · Thai rice dishes · Grilled meat + potato anywhere.
06
Training vs Rest Days
Training days: all 3 meals + snack. Rest days: eat when hungry, always keep the protein snack. Don't skip protein even if not very hungry.

Core Principles
01
Hip Thrusts, Every Week, Always Heavier
The single movement most directly responsible for glute shape. Add load every 1–2 weeks without exception. 1.25 kg counts.
02
Train the Glute Med for Width
Clamshells and abduction work = hip width. Most programs skip this. It's the difference between a round vs flat silhouette from the front.
03
Stretch Hip Flexors Daily
Tight hip flexors prevent glutes from firing. Your yoga practice is an actual asset here. Pigeon + low lunge every day.
04
Mornings Are Your Window
Babylonia owns your afternoons. Train 8–11 AM. Build the morning habit in June and it sustains through the full 12 weeks.
05
Tresor Night = Recovery Morning
You're on your feet for hours on a shift. That's physical load. Yoga + walk the morning after. Sleep wins over one session.
06
Measurements Over Scale
Muscle is denser than fat. You may gain weight on the scale while visually transforming. Measure hips and thighs weekly.
07
Pre-Portion Before Smoking
Bowl of snack, portioned, sitting on the counter. Done before you smoke. This one habit is worth more than any other diet rule.
08
HRT + Training Compound
Estrogen redistributes fat to hips and thighs. The muscle you're building gives that redistribution its shape — roundness, not just softness.
